## Pre-purchase equestrian property consultations

ELD offers a pre-purchase consultation service for buyers considering an equestrian property. This service is designed to assess whether a property is suitable for development into a functional equestrian property or facility, before you commit to purchase.

Consultations may consider:

- Arena and stable location potential
- Flood, drainage, and soil considerations
- Earthworks requirements and constraints
- Practical movement of horses and vehicles
- Environmental and long-term usability factors

This service provides clarity and confidence when purchasing land for equestrian use.

## Independent, practical advice

Unlike traditional property assessments, ELD's advice is grounded in real-world equestrian construction and property development experience. Our goal is to help clients avoid costly mistakes and understand the true potential – and limitations – of a property.

## Supporting smarter equestrian property decisions

Whether you are buying, selling, or assessing equestrian properties, ELD provides informed, practical support backed by decades of industry experience.

::: callout
### Verified ELD arena construction records

ELD has maintained detailed records of all arena constructions completed since 2000. If you are considering the purchase of an equestrian property advertised as having an ELD-constructed arena, we encourage you to contact us to verify the details.

By checking our construction records, buyers can:

- Confirm the arena was genuinely built by ELD
- Understand the original construction method and scope
- Gain confidence in the quality and authenticity of the arena

This service helps ensure you are getting the real deal, rather than relying solely on marketing claims.
:::
