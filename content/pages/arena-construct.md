In early 2000, Todd Cunningham designed and constructed the first ELD Full Spec. Arena. ELD has been constructing on average one arena per week since establishment and has developed specialised equipment, machinery, materials and processes specifically for equestrian arena construction.

Each arena is an individual, which must suit our client's requirements, chosen discipline and also fit aesthetically into the property.
