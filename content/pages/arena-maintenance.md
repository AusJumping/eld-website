## Why arena maintenance matters

**Protect your investment and prolong your surface life.**

At ELD, we've long emphasised to our clients that regular arena maintenance is essential to protect the long-term performance and longevity of your investment.

Just as a vehicle requires more frequent servicing the more it's driven, the frequency of your arena use directly impacts how often it should be maintained. Heavily used surfaces require more attention than those used occasionally – neglecting this can lead to costly repairs or premature resurfacing.

## Extend the life of your arena surface

On average, a well-constructed arena surface has an expected life of **5 to 7 years**, depending on usage and maintenance. With consistent care – both routine and professional – this lifespan can be extended significantly. Regular maintenance helps prevent surface deterioration, protects your base, and ensures a safe and consistent ride for horse and rider.

## Arena maintenance options

### Daily & weekly maintenance tips

Daily/weekly maintenance routines are vital. Using the right tools ensures your arena base remains undisturbed.

- **Avoid generic implements.** Poorly suited equipment can damage the carefully built base beneath your surface.
- **Use purpose-built tools.** The [ELD Arena Spread-a-bar®](/shop/), designed by Todd Cunningham, is engineered specifically to maintain the quality and integrity of your arena surface.

### Professional arena maintenance visits

We recommend booking an **ELD Maintenance Visit every 3, 6, or 12 months**, depending on the level of use.

Our visits include use of our custom-designed **Automated Laser Planes**, which restore the surface to its original base modelling depths – ensuring consistent footing and accurate levels across the entire arena.
