ELD is pleased to offer the following Arena Maintenance Solution to assist in ensuring the longevity of your arena or work surface.

## ELD Arena Spread-a-bar®

The E.L.D Arena Spread-a-bar® has proven to be the popular choice when it comes to maintaining your arena surface.

Designed and engineered specifically to retain depth of surface, it has a flat edge on one side and in-built harrows on the reverse, ideal for tilling and loosening compacted surfaces.

The ELD Arena Spread-a-bar® can be used on a variety of arena surfaces.

This design also enables you to drag paddocks and spread manure with great ease.

::: figure spread-a-bar | The ELD Arena Spread-a-bar®.
:::

It comes with a simple D-ring for hitching to your chosen towing vehicle which can be mower, bike, utility or tractor. Or you can simply loop the chain over your towball.

Ideal for both indoor and outdoor use the E.L.D Arena Spread-a-bar® has an easy fold away system for storage.

Designed by Todd Cunningham and **Made in Australia** by our Brisbane based manufacturers.

The E.L.D Arena Spread-a-bar® is available in three sizes or can be made to order upon request.
