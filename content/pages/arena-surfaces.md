## Natural manufactured sand surfaces for Australian equestrian facilities

We specialise in equestrian arena construction in Australia, using our own manufactured sand surfaces to create high-performance, environmentally responsible riding arenas. Horses are naturally designed to work on natural ground surfaces, and our sand arenas reflect this – providing consistent footing, rider confidence, and long-term reliability without the environmental concerns associated with synthetic surfaces.

## Why we use manufactured sand instead of synthetic surfaces

Synthetic equestrian surfaces often contain plastics, fibres, or rubber that can break down over time and pose environmental risks. In contrast, our Australian-manufactured sand arena surfaces are completely natural, making them a more sustainable choice for riding arenas, training facilities and equestrian centres.

By producing our own sand, we ensure:

- Consistent grading and quality
- Reduced transport emissions
- A surface aligned with natural equine movement

Synthetic surface tips: [read about synthetic surfaces](/maintenance-surface-products/surface-products/).

## Designed for Australian conditions

Our manufactured sand surfaces are specifically suited to Australia's climate, offering excellent dust control and moisture retention when used with arena watering and irrigation systems.

Key benefits include:

- Easy dust management during dry conditions
- Efficient response to watering systems
- Simple, low-cost ongoing maintenance

This makes our sand arenas ideal for both indoor and outdoor equestrian arena constructions.

## Easy maintenance, long-term performance

Manufactured sand riding arenas are straightforward to maintain, requiring only routine watering and levelling. Without synthetic additives, there are no fibre top-ups or complex replacement processes – delivering long-term value and environmental peace of mind.
