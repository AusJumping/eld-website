## Dressage arena construction

In early 2000, Todd Cunningham designed and constructed the first ELD Full Spec. Dressage Arena. This design consisted of two base layers, ELD Surface, Sleeper Surrounds and all earthworks included and to this day has proven to be successful and long lasting.

ELD has been constructing on average one arena per week since establishment and has over the years developed specialised equipment, machinery, materials and processes specifically for equestrian arena construction.

Each arena is an individual, which must suit our client's requirements, chosen discipline and also fit aesthetically into the property.

ELD offers a range of sand arena surfaces suitable for Australian conditions. Consideration for the surrounding environment and horse and rider health is also a priority.

## Dressage arena rebuild & restoration

A number of clients come to ELD for rebuild, restoration and renovation of poorly, cheaply and incorrectly constructed arenas. A number of constructions are designed using processes that are more suited to European conditions. These may include drainage systems underneath the arena, incorrect use of geotextiles and other materials which cannot always cope with Australian weather conditions and soil types.

If you have an existing arena that is not performing to its full potential, ELD provides a restoration service that includes reconstruction, resurfacing and developing improved drainage systems.
