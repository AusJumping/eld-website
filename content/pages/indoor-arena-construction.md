## Proven experience in large-scale indoor arena projects

ELD brings extensive experience to indoor equestrian arena construction in Australia, with a strong background in delivering large, complex equestrian facilities. Historically, ELD operated two specialist construction arms – **ELD Sheds & Stables** and **Todd Cunningham Projects** – which together delivered a significant number of indoor arena and equestrian building projects across Australia.

This hands-on construction background provides ELD with a deep understanding of structural requirements, arena functionality and the practical challenges involved in building large indoor equestrian facilities.

## Guidance from concept to completion

With decades of combined experience, ELD is well positioned to assist clients through every stage of the indoor arena construction process. From early planning and design considerations through to construction coordination and final delivery, we provide informed, practical guidance to ensure your project runs smoothly.

ELD works collaboratively with your chosen shed or steel building contractor, acting as an experienced advisor and project manager to help integrate the arena structure, footing, drainage, and riding surface into a cohesive, high-performing facility.

## Project management for large equestrian constructions

ELD has vast experience in project managing large-scale equestrian and agricultural construction projects, ensuring timelines, budgets, and quality standards are maintained. Our understanding of both equestrian requirements and commercial construction processes allows us to bridge the gap between builders, suppliers, and clients.

Key benefits of working with ELD include:

- Practical insight gained from direct construction experience
- Coordination with shed and arena construction companies
- Risk mitigation through informed planning and oversight
- Efficient delivery of complex indoor arena projects

## Trusted indoor arena construction support

Whether you are building a private indoor riding arena or a large commercial equestrian facility, ELD offers the experience, knowledge, and project management capability to support successful outcomes.

For indoor equestrian arena construction in Australia, ELD provides reliable expertise built on real-world construction experience.
