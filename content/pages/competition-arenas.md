ELD have been involved in the design, construct and supply of surface to arenas for both public and privately owned competition venues.

As a number of clubs are dependent on Grants and Funding, Leased Land and often sharing facilities with other disciplines, ELD take into consideration these sometimes lengthy processes when consulting with the committees.

ELD takes into account the requirements for the specified discipline. Consideration of the FEI guidelines for sizing, distances between arenas and selecting appropriate locations as far as access for the judges, spectators and sponsors at events.
