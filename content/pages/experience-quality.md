## Our story

Equestrian Land Developments (ELD) was born from a simple request: a family friend needed a custom-built equestrian arena. From this first project, the **ELD Spec. Arena** – featuring a Base Layer, Blinding Layer, and premium Surface with Sleeper Surrounds – was created. Word of mouth quickly grew our reputation and in 2000, Equestrian Land Developments Pty Ltd was officially established.

Since then, ELD has built, on average, one arena per week for over 25 years, becoming a trusted name in arena construction and equestrian property development across Australia.

### Meet Todd Cunningham – Founder & Director

Todd Cunningham, our founder, has spent his life immersed in the world of horses. Growing up on a property with spelling stables accommodating over 120 horses, Todd gained an in-depth understanding of horse health, safety, and optimal property layout. His father, Master Farrier Claye Cunningham, instilled a passion for equine care, while Todd's career in earthmoving and construction – including international experience in underwater excavation and road building – equipped him with unique expertise to combine horse knowledge with precision earthworks.

Today, Todd has overseen a wide range of construction projects, both in Australia and abroad, bringing unparalleled experience to every project.
:::

## Arena design & property planning

Choosing the right location and design for your arena is crucial. ELD provides custom design advice for optimum arena placement, functionality and aesthetics, tailored to your property and equestrian discipline. Our expertise ensures your investment is safe, practical, and visually appealing.

## Commitment to quality & innovation

At ELD, our premium natural arena surfaces, materials and maintenance solutions form the foundation of our business. We continually undertake research and development locally and internationally to stay at the forefront of equestrian arena construction innovation.

As an Australian family-owned business, we combine professional project management with a personal touch. We take pride in:

- Accountability and craftsmanship
- High-quality, durable construction
- Supporting equestrian sports through sponsorships and community engagement
