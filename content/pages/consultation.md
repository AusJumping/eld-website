Equestrian Land Developments (ELD), based in South East Queensland, specialises in arena construction, rebuild, and restoration for all equestrian disciplines. With decades of experience, Todd Cunningham and the ELD team provide practical, site-specific guidance to ensure your arena or equestrian property is functional, durable, and tailored to your needs.

## Onsite consultation & pre-purchase inspections

ELD offers onsite consultations to assess your property, review site layout and explain the full arena construction process. This is also the ideal opportunity to ask questions about your project and gain professional advice on:

- Arena design and surface selection
- Site drainage and earthworks
- Property layout and future equestrian facility planning

We also provide [pre-purchase property inspections](/real-estate-equestrian-land-developments/) to evaluate the suitability of a property for equestrian use. **Fees apply for onsite consultations, which are credited toward your arena construction if you proceed.**

## Full-spec arena construction

Todd Cunningham personally oversees every arena project. Full-spec ELD arenas include:

- Two engineered base layers
- ELD surface
- Sleeper surrounds
- Earthworks included

Construction is generally completed in **3–4 days** and is **ready to ride immediately**. Pricing varies based on earthworks, property location, and site requirements:

::: pricing
20m x 60m Arena | $70,000 – $80,000* + GST
20m x 40m Arena | $60,000 – $70,000* + GST
:::

_*Current pricing as at 01/08/2025, subject to change._

## Verified ELD arena records

ELD maintains records of all arenas constructed since 2000. If a property is advertised with an ELD-constructed arena, contact us to verify authenticity and ensure you're getting the real deal.
