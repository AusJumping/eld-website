## Larapinta Equestrian, Queensland

ELD constructed a Polo Field at Larapinta Equestrian for the Irvine Family in SE QLD. Included in this project was design of two fully turfed Polo fields, Stable Building and Horse facilities, Truck and Visitor parking, roadways, catering areas and viewing mound.
