How much does an ELD arena cost?
A full-spec ELD **20m x 60m arena** is **$70,000 – $80,000 + GST** and a **20m x 40m arena** is **$60,000 – $70,000 + GST** (current pricing as at 01/08/2025, subject to change). Pricing varies based on earthworks, property location and site requirements. [See consultation & quotation](/arena-construct/eld-construction-quotation-process/).

How long does it take to build an arena?
Construction of a full-spec ELD arena is generally completed in **3–4 days**, and the arena is **ready to ride immediately**.

What is included in a full-spec ELD arena?
Two engineered base layers, the ELD surface, sleeper surrounds and earthworks. Todd Cunningham personally oversees every arena project.

Do you use synthetic arena surfaces?
No. ELD has made a clear commitment to move away from synthetic additives in all arena surfaces because of concerns around microfibres, microplastics, toxins leaching into soil and waterways, heat absorption and end-of-life removal costs. ELD exclusively uses its own natural manufactured sand surfaces. [Read more](/maintenance-surface-products/surface-products/).

Where is ELD based and which areas do you service?
ELD is based in South East Queensland (postal address: PO Box 98, Cooroy QLD 4563). Arena constructions have been completed across Queensland and New South Wales, and Todd has overseen projects in Australia and abroad. Arena maintenance visits currently service **SE QLD and Northern NSW**.

How long does an arena surface last?
On average, a well-constructed arena surface has an expected life of **5 to 7 years**, depending on usage and maintenance. With consistent routine and professional care, this lifespan can be extended significantly.

How often should I book an arena maintenance visit?
We recommend an ELD Maintenance Visit every **3, 6 or 12 months**, depending on the level of use. Visits use ELD's custom-designed Automated Laser Planes to restore the surface to its original base modelling depths. Lead times are a minimum of 4–6 weeks from booking.

Is there a fee for an onsite consultation?
Yes, fees apply for onsite consultations, and they are **credited toward your arena construction** if you proceed.

Can you rebuild or restore an arena built by someone else?
Yes. Many clients come to ELD to rebuild, restore and renovate poorly, cheaply or incorrectly constructed arenas – including reconstruction, resurfacing and improved drainage. [See rebuild & restoration](/arena-construct/dressage-arenas/#dressage-arena-rebuild-restoration).

Can you confirm whether an arena was really built by ELD?
Yes. ELD has kept records of every arena constructed since 2000. If a property is advertised with an ELD-constructed arena, contact us to verify it.

Do you build indoor arenas?
ELD works with your chosen shed or steel building contractor as an experienced advisor and project manager, integrating the arena structure, footing, drainage and riding surface. Historically ELD delivered many indoor projects through ELD Sheds & Stables and Todd Cunningham Projects. [Indoor arena construction](/shed-construction/indoor-arena-construction-equestrian-land-developments/).

Can you build an arena on flood-prone land?
Yes. ELD has extensive experience building on flood-prone properties and considers arena positioning and elevation, drainage and water flow, surface and base construction methods, and post-flood access. [Flood prone properties](/arena-construct/flood-prone-areas/).

What disciplines do you build for?
Dressage, show jumping and eventing, campdraft and reining (Western), competition and temporary competition arenas, polo and polocrosse fields, round yards, lunging rings and walker pads.

It has a flat edge on one side and 30mm harrow tines on the other, has no wheels, folds away for storage and can be towed by a mower, bike, ute or tractor.
