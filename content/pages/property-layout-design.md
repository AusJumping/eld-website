## Practical, climate-responsive and environmentally smart property planning

ELD specialises in equestrian property design and layout, creating practical, efficient properties tailored to the location, climate and day-to-day operation of both horses and people. Every design is approached with a clear understanding of how an equestrian property functions, ensuring safe, logical movement throughout the site.

## Designed for Australian conditions

We design equestrian properties to suit local climate, landform, drainage, and orientation, improving usability year-round while reducing long-term maintenance. Thoughtful planning of arenas, stables, paddocks, and access points helps minimise dust, heat exposure and erosion.

## Practical movement for horses and clients

ELD focuses on efficient layout and flow, allowing horses, riders, and staff to move around the property in the most practical and stress-free way. Well-planned positioning of facilities improves safety, daily efficiency and overall functionality.

## Environmentally smart equestrian design

Environmental responsibility is central to our approach. We prioritise natural materials, sustainable surface choices, efficient water use and minimal land disturbance, delivering equestrian properties that work in harmony with their surroundings.

## Experience you can trust

Backed by extensive experience in equestrian construction and project delivery, ELD provides informed, practical design solutions that are functional, durable, and future-focused.
