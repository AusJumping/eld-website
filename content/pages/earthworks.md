## Experienced, practical earthworks for equestrian properties

ELD provides specialist equestrian property earthworks, delivering practical, well-planned groundworks that form the foundation of successful equestrian facilities. With a strong understanding of how equestrian properties function, we ensure earthworks are completed with long-term usability, drainage, and environmental responsibility in mind.

## Complete equestrian earthworks services

ELD can assist with all aspects of equestrian earthworks, including:

::: features
Dams | Practical water storage and drainage solutions.
Roads & driveways | Durable access for horse floats, trucks, and daily use.
Stable pads | Correctly prepared pads for long-term structural integrity.
Land levelling | Precise shaping for arenas, yards, and paddocks.
:::

Each project is approached with careful consideration of property layout, horse movement, and ongoing maintenance requirements.

## Proven experience in large-scale earthworks

Prior to establishing ELD in 2000, Todd gained extensive international experience working on major earthmoving and construction projects. His background includes road construction, foreman roles on large-scale subdivisions and shopping centre developments, and involvement in highly specialised underwater excavation projects.

This depth of experience translates into a strong understanding of complex site conditions, precision earthworks and efficient project delivery – skills that directly benefit equestrian property development.

## Earthworks that set your property up for success

With a combination of large-scale construction expertise and equestrian-specific knowledge, ELD delivers earthworks solutions that are practical, durable, and fit for purpose.
