What is ELD doing for the environment? / sustainability / eco / trees / environmentally friendly / microplastics
ELD uses only natural manufactured sand surfaces (no synthetic additives), manages earthworks to prevent erosion and runoff, and contributes a portion of revenue to Sea Shepherd, Greenfleet, OzHarvest and The Great Barrier Reef Foundation. A little over 600 trees had been planted on the ELD home property by the end of 2021. [Our commitment](/environmental-sustainability-commitment/).

Who is Todd Cunningham? / founder / owner / who runs ELD / about ELD / history
Todd Cunningham founded Equestrian Land Developments in 2000. He grew up on a property with spelling stables for over 120 horses, his father is Master Farrier Claye Cunningham, and his background is in earthmoving and construction, including international underwater excavation and road building. ELD has built, on average, one arena per week for over 25 years. [About us](/experience-quality/).

Can you design my whole property? / property layout / property design / stables / paddocks / earthworks / dams / driveways
Yes. ELD designs equestrian property layouts to suit your climate, landform, drainage and the day-to-day movement of horses and people, and handles earthworks including dams, roads & driveways, stable pads and land levelling. [Property development](/property-development/).
