With border restrictions finally lifting we were able to head across the border to a client who has been waiting all year!

A couple of rather large projects were completed and as the run into the end of 2020 begins we begin to plan for 2021.

## Arena constructions

- 35m x 70m Reining Arena Rebuild (Not ELD Constructed), Coolgardie, NSW.
- Scarifying & Top Dressing Polo Fields, Larapinta, QLD.
- Polo Field Extensions, Field #2, Larapinta, QLD.
- Completion of 40m x 80m Indoor Reining Arena (90m x 50m Pad, 84m x 44m Base, 70m x 35m Riding Surface). Cattle Area/Shed Pad 25m x 50m. Maudsland, QLD.
- 40m x 80m Arena Construction & Shed Floor, Windera, QLD.
- 20m x 40m Dressage Arena Construction, Bald Knob, QLD.

## Arena maintenance visits

- 20m x 60m ELD Construction 2019, Eerwah Vale, QLD.
